Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Fees and Transaction Limits

## Transaction fees
- Some financial services may have service fees.
- Applicable fees are displayed in the application before a transaction is confirmed whenever possible.
- Fees may vary depending on the transaction type, destination, currency, account type, and payment method.

## Transfer limits
- Transfer limits may apply on a per-transaction, daily, or monthly basis.
- Customers can view applicable limits in the application when the feature is available.
- A transaction may be declined if it exceeds an applicable limit.

## ATM fees
- ATM operators may charge their own fees.
- Any applicable fintech service fee is separate from an ATM operator fee.

## Currency conversion
- Currency conversion may involve an exchange rate and, depending on the service, an additional fee or markup.
- The applicable rate and charges should be reviewed before confirming a foreign-currency transaction.
