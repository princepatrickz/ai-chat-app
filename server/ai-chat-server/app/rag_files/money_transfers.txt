Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Money Transfers

## Sending money
- Customers can initiate transfers from the Transfers section of the application.
- Before confirming a transfer, customers should verify the recipient name, account number or identifier, amount, and destination.
- A transfer cannot always be canceled after it has been submitted.

## Transfer status
- A transfer may show statuses such as Pending, Completed, Failed, or Reversed.
- Pending transfers are still being processed.
- Completed transfers have been successfully processed according to the transaction status.
- Failed transfers were not successfully processed.
- Reversed transfers were returned or canceled after processing.

## Transfer pending
- If a transfer remains pending beyond the estimated processing period shown in the app, contact support.
- Support may request the transaction reference number.

## Wrong recipient
- Customers should verify recipient information before confirming a transfer.
- If money was sent to the wrong recipient, contact support as soon as possible.
- Recovery is not guaranteed because the ability to reverse a transfer depends on its processing status and applicable rules.

## Transfer limits
- Daily and monthly transfer limits may apply.
- Limits can depend on account status, verification level, transfer type, and applicable risk controls.

## Canceling transfer
- A transfer cannot always be canceled after it has been submitted.
- If a customer needs help with a submitted transfer, they should contact support as soon as possible.
- Cancellation is not guaranteed.
