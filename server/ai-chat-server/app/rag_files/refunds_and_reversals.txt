Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Refunds and Reversals

## Merchant refund
- A merchant may issue a refund for an eligible card purchase.
- Once the merchant processes the refund, it may take several business days to appear in the customer's account.
- Customers should retain the merchant's refund confirmation when available.

## Reversed transaction
- A reversed transaction means a transaction that was previously authorized or processed has been returned or canceled.
- The returned amount may take additional processing time before becoming available.

## Pending card transaction
- A pending card transaction may represent an authorization that has not yet been finalized.
- Some pending authorizations disappear automatically if the merchant does not complete the transaction.
- If a pending transaction remains for an unusually long period, contact support.

## Refund not received
- If a merchant confirms a refund but the funds have not appeared after the expected processing period, contact support.
- Provide the merchant refund reference or transaction details when available.
