Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Customer Support

## Support hours
- Live customer support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Philippine Time (PHT).
- Support may have different hours during public holidays.

## Contacting support
- Customers can contact support through Hotline: (02) 8250 4848 or support portal .
- Transaction-related requests should include the transaction reference when available.
- Customers should never include passwords, OTPs, PINs, or full card security information in a support request.

## Security
- Support representatives will never ask customers to reveal their password, OTP, PIN, or full card security code.
- Customers should use only official support channels.
- If a customer receives a suspicious message claiming to be from the fintech company, they should report it through the official support channel.
