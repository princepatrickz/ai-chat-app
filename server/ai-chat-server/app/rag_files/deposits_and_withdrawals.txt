Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Deposits and Withdrawals

## Adding money
- Customers can add money using supported funding methods shown in the application.
- The available funding methods may vary by country and account type.
- A deposit may remain pending while the funding provider processes the transaction.

## Withdrawal
- Customers can request a withdrawal from their available balance.
- The withdrawal destination must be supported and may require verification.
- Processing times depend on the withdrawal method and provider.

## Cash withdrawal
- If ATM withdrawals are supported, customers should use an eligible card and ATM.
- ATM fees may be charged by the ATM operator or service provider.
- If cash was not received but the account was charged, contact support with the ATM location, date, time, and transaction amount.

## Deposit not received
- If a completed deposit is not reflected in the available balance after the expected processing period, contact support.
- Include the transaction reference and amount when contacting support.
