Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Accounts and Registration

## Opening an account
- Customers can create a personal account through the mobile app.
- A valid email address and mobile phone number are required.
- Customers must complete identity verification before using certain financial services.
- One person should not create multiple personal accounts unless specifically permitted by the company.

## Identity verification
- Customers may be asked to provide their full name, date of birth, residential address, government-issued identification, and a selfie.
- Verification normally takes a few minutes, but manual review can take up to 2 business days.
- A verification request may be rejected if the submitted information is unclear, expired, incomplete, or inconsistent.

## Changing personal information
- Customers can update certain profile information from Settings > Personal Information.
- Changes to sensitive information may require additional identity verification.
- Customers should contact support if they cannot update their information in the app.

## Account closure
- Customers can request account closure through the support center.
- Pending transactions, unresolved disputes, or remaining balances may need to be completed before an account can be closed.
