Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Fraud, Unauthorized Transactions, and Disputes

## Unauthorized transaction
- Customers should report transactions they do not recognize as soon as possible.
- Customers should temporarily freeze affected cards or account features when the application provides that option.
- Support may ask questions to verify the customer's identity and investigate the transaction.

## Fraud warning signs
- Customers should be cautious of messages requesting passwords, OTPs, PINs, card security codes, or remote access to their device.
- A legitimate support representative will not ask for a customer's password or OTP.
- Customers should access the service through the official application or website rather than links received from unknown senders.

## Dispute investigation
- Support may request transaction details, screenshots, receipts, or other evidence.
- Investigation time varies depending on transaction type and the information available.
- A submitted dispute does not automatically guarantee a refund.

## Chargeback
- Some eligible card transactions may be disputed through a card chargeback process.
- Eligibility and deadlines depend on the transaction type, payment network, and applicable rules.
