Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Sample Financial Products

## Flex Account
- Flex Account is a fictional digital account used for this sample RAG dataset.
- Customers can hold an available balance and use supported transfer and payment features.
- Identity verification is required before certain features become available.

## Flex Card
- Flex Card is a fictional debit card linked to the customer's Flex Account.
- Customers can use the card for eligible purchases and supported ATM withdrawals.
- A physical Flex Card requires activation before normal use.

## Flex Transfer
- Flex Transfer is a fictional money-transfer feature.
- Customers can send money to supported recipients from the application.
- Transfer limits and processing times depend on the transaction type and account status.

## Important
- These products are fictional and exist only for testing the RAG application.
- Do not treat the sample information as real financial-product terms or financial advice.
