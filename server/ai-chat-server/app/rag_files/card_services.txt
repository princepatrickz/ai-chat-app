Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Debit and Virtual Cards

## Card activation
- A physical card must be activated before it can normally be used for purchases.
- Card activation instructions are provided in the mobile application.

## Card payment declined
- Check that the card is active and has sufficient available balance.
- Verify that the card has not expired and that the transaction details are correct.
- Some merchants or transaction types may be restricted.
- If the problem continues, contact support.

## Lost or stolen card
- Customers should immediately freeze or lock a lost or stolen card using the mobile application when that feature is available.
- Customers should then contact support to report the card.
- A replacement card may be issued according to the applicable replacement policy.

## Card payment dispute
- Customers should report an unauthorized card transaction as soon as possible.
- Support may request the transaction date, amount, merchant name, and other verification information.
- Customers should never provide their full card PIN or account password to support.

## Virtual cards
- A virtual card can be used for eligible online purchases.
- Virtual card details should be treated as confidential financial information.
