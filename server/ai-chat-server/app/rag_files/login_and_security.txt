Fintech Customer Support Knowledge Base
Topic: Login and Account Security

## Forgotten password
- Select "Forgot Password" on the login screen.
- Enter the registered email address or mobile number.
- Follow the password-reset instructions sent by the service.
- Never share a password or one-time password (OTP) with another person.

## One-time passwords
- OTPs are used to verify certain login and financial actions.
- An OTP should only be entered into the official fintech application or website.
- Support agents will never ask a customer to provide an OTP.

## Account locked
- An account may be temporarily locked after repeated unsuccessful login attempts.
- Customers should wait for the lockout period to expire or follow the recovery instructions shown in the application.
- Customers who believe their account has been compromised should immediately change their password and contact support.

## Suspected account takeover
- If a customer notices an unfamiliar login, transfer, card transaction, or profile change, they should contact support immediately.
- Customers should not continue communicating with a person who claims to be support and requests passwords, OTPs, PINs, or full card details.
